Case studies

How MedShield brings inflated medical costs back to market rate and gets crew care back on track - across cruise, shipping and offshore. Real cases, real figures, taken verbatim from the files.

  1. A hospital tripled its prices. We brought them back.

    Cost optimisation · India

    Once the hospital learned an employer was covering costs, consultation fees were billed at 3× market rate and surgery quoted at 3.8×. We negotiated every line back to market through our provider network and monitored the file through to settlement.

    66% total claim reduced

  2. The wrong hospital cost 40% more recovery time.

    Provider selection · India

    A PEME vendor referred a seafarer on a financial relationship rather than clinical fit. We re-shortlisted providers on our scored index, had specialists formally review the original treatment records, and secured an immediate appointment through a Case Management Specialist.

    3.5L INR saved, recovery back on track

  3. Rehabilitation moved outside the hospital's margin

    Strategic referral · India

    Pre-habilitation therapy had been outsourced to a third party inside the hospital's own premises, with the hospital's margin added on top. We referred the seafarer to independent specialists and costed the programme against market rate.

    60% therapy cost reduction

  4. A crew case handled from the berth, not the invoice

    Cruise · port coordination

    A crew member landed ashore mid-itinerary with the vessel due to sail. We took the case at the gangway - provider selected, treatment costed and approved before delivery, and repatriation documented against MLC obligations as the case ran.

    30% faster claim turnaround

  5. Consultation fees brought back in line with market rates

    Cost negotiation · India

    Charging 1.5× to 10× the market average is an unregulated practice across Indian and Asian healthcare. Every consultation on the file was re-costed against our pre-negotiated rates before it was approved.

    65% consultation fees reduced
