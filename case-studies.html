Case 01 — Cost optimisation, India

A hospital tripled its prices. We brought them back.

The problem
Once the hospital learned an employer was covering costs, consultation fees were billed at 3× market rate, pre-habilitation at 2.5×, and surgery quoted at 3.8×. Unregulated pricing in the region allows markups of 1.5× to 10× the average - and outsourced services within the hospital carried additional fees on top.
What we did
Negotiated rates back to market using our provider network, moved rehabilitation to independent specialists outside the hospital's margin, maintained transparent communication throughout, and monitored every line to settlement.

Result

2.55L

Surgery cost, down from INR 7.75 lakh

65%

Consultation fees reduced

60%

Rehabilitation costs reduced

66%+

Total claim reduced

Key takeaway: cost negotiation, strategic referrals and transparent communication prevented significant overcharges across every stage of care.

Case 02 — Provider selection, India

The wrong hospital cost 40% more recovery time.

The problem
With no provider arrangement in India, an employer relied on a PEME vendor who referred the seafarer to a facility based on a financial relationship rather than clinical fit. The facility lacked expertise and infrastructure, resulting in substandard care and recovery running 40% longer than expected.
What we did
Re-shortlisted providers on our scored index, deployed specialists to formally review the original treatment records and challenge the initial plan, secured an immediate appointment through a Case Management Specialist, and delivered a detailed report to the employer outlining the deficiencies.

Result

3.5L

INR saved on cost

✓

Recovery back on track

Key takeaway: unbiased provider selection, ethical oversight and proactive problem-solving improved outcomes and restored trust - the value of a partner with no conflict of interest.
