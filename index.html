The problem

The six questions that start every medical case at sea

Every crew medical case opens the same way: with doubt on the bridge and no one ashore who owns the answer. These six questions decide what the case costs — in hours, in money, and in outcome.

  1. 01

    Who do we call?

    It is 03:00 and the nearest coast station is out of VHF range. No one on board knows which number reaches a doctor.

  2. 02

    Is it serious?

    Chest pain, or indigestion after a long watch? Nobody aboard is qualified to tell the difference - and guessing wrong costs hours that matter.

  3. 03

    Do we divert?

    A diversion burns fuel, breaks the charter and costs days. Not diverting could cost a life. That call sits with the master, alone, at sea.

  4. 04

    Which port?

    The closest harbour is not always the one with a cardiac unit, a surgeon on duty, or clearance to take a foreign crew member ashore.

  5. 05

    Are we being overcharged?

    Once a hospital learns an employer is paying, prices move. We have seen consultation fees at three times the market rate and surgery quoted at nearly four.

  6. 06

    Who chose this hospital?

    If the referral came from a vendor with a financial relationship rather than a clinical one, recovery takes longer and costs more - and nobody ashore finds out until the invoice arrives.

Services

What we do

  1. 01

    Claims management

    You get: a claim documented from the first hour, so it settles instead of being disputed.

    End-to-end handling from incident documentation and evidence collection through employer negotiation to final settlement. Covers crew injury, illness, and lifestyle and mental health conditions - each handled against maritime law and international compliance standards.

  2. 02

    Claims advisory & legal

    You get: a defensible position before a case becomes a liability.

    Advisory across medical, disability and life claims. Structured review of the employer's obligations, medical records and documentation; collaborative resolution between crew, employer and agents; and alignment with MLC and ILO standards. Our medico-legal team mediates disputes to fair resolution rather than litigating them.

  3. 03

    Medical welfare budgeting

    You get: a crew medical spend you can forecast at budget time and defend at year end.

    Needs assessment across your fleet, a budget designed around preventive care, evacuation contingency and telemedicine costs, then data-driven forecasting to remove financial surprise. Built to satisfy global maritime health regulation.

  4. 04

    Telemedicine

    You get: a doctor on the line before the master has to make a diversion call alone.

    24/7 access to licensed doctors and specialists, supported by video consultation and wearable diagnostics, so vessels avoid unnecessary evacuations and port diversions - the single most expensive outcome of an unmanaged case.

Also: Occupational Health & Safety - maritime safety audits, PEME and vessel-specific audits - and Medico-Legal Services for injury claims and regulatory compliance.

How a case runs

One case, eight steps, one point of contact

  1. 01

    The case opens

    A seafarer needs medical support. One number, answered at any hour.

  2. 02

    A dedicated CMS is assigned

    One named Case Management Specialist becomes the single liaison between cruise line, agent, hospital and patient.

  3. 03

    Our SME panel reviews

    Specialists assess urgency, complexity and treatment options - so decisions are backed by expertise, not guesswork.

  4. 04

    Hospital selection, scored

    A multi-parameter index ranks facilities on expertise, success rate, cost, accessibility and NABH / NABL / ISO accreditation.

  5. 05

    Treatment plan approved

    Plan, expected outcome and financial estimate agreed with the employer before treatment begins.

  6. 06

    International payment handled

    The hospital is paid directly, documented and compliant - no crew member out of pocket, no port agent float.

  7. 07

    Recovery monitored

    Oversight continues through rehabilitation and follow-up, with the employer updated throughout.

  8. 08

    Fitness report & dashboard

    A treating doctor's fitness-to-work report, and every record, approval and transaction on one secure dashboard.

The dashboard means you are never asking us for an update - approvals, payments and reports are already there.

A hospital tripled its prices. We brought them back.

The problem
Once the hospital learned an employer was covering costs, consultation fees were billed at 3× market rate, pre-habilitation at 2.5×, and surgery quoted at 3.8×.
What we did
Negotiated rates back to market using our provider network, moved rehabilitation to independent specialists outside the hospital's margin, and monitored every line to settlement.
Result
Surgery reduced from INR 7.75 lakh to INR 2.55 lakh. Consultation fees down 65%, rehabilitation down 60%, and the total claim reduced by over 66%.

The wrong hospital cost 40% more recovery time.

The problem
With no provider arrangement in India, an employer relied on a PEME vendor who referred the seafarer to a facility based on a financial relationship rather than clinical fit. Recovery ran 40% longer than expected.
What we did
Re-shortlisted providers on our scored index, had our specialists formally review the original treatment records, secured an immediate appointment through a Case Management Specialist, and reported the deficiencies to the employer.
Result
Approximately INR 3.5 lakh saved, recovery back on track, and the seafarer spared further complications.
