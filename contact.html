FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

How fast do you respond?

A case manager replies to every proposal request within one business day. For a medical emergency at sea, do not use the form - call +91 98406 03936, answered by a coordinator.

Do you cover vessels outside India, Singapore and the UAE?

We operate from 45+ locations across India, with offices in Singapore and the UAE, and a partner network of 1,800+ hospitals, clinics, labs, pharmacies and rehabilitation providers. Telemedicine reaches your crew wherever the vessel is, so a case can begin at sea regardless of position and be handed to a treating facility on arrival. Outside that network we coordinate with local providers at the nearest port of call.

Can you take over claims that are already in progress?

Every case is assigned a dedicated Case Management Specialist who acts as the single liaison between owner, agent, crew member and hospital, and who monitors the case against its timelines. Our SME panel then reviews the medical position and the treatment plan, which is the same first step whether a case starts with us or reaches us part-way through. Talk to us about the case and we will tell you what we can pick up.

What does an annual medical welfare budget include?

We begin with a needs assessment of your current provisions, then build the budget around them: preventive care and health monitoring, contingency for medical evacuation and emergencies, and telemedicine and onboard medical staffing costs. Data-driven forecasting covers the contingencies, and the framework is checked against the maritime health regulations that apply to your fleet.

How is telemedicine delivered to a vessel at sea?

Your vessel is connected to licensed doctors and specialists around the clock, for anything from a routine consultation to urgent care. Video-enabled consultations and wearable diagnostic devices let the onboard team work directly with shore-based clinicians, which resolves cases early and reduces costly evacuations and port diversions.

MedShield manages crew medical claims for owners who treat seafarer health as seriously as the vessel itself.
